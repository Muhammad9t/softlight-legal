01

Overview

Day & Night Live Wallpaper, published by Softlight Studio Apps, is a living landscape wallpaper that follows the real sun, moon and weather where you are, with a customizable clock and your own photos. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app.

The short version: there are no accounts and no servers of ours. Your photos and settings never leave your device. Your location is used on your device to place the sun and moon, and is sent only to our weather provider to get your local forecast. We don't sell your data. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID (see below). Day & Night Pro removes ads entirely.

02

Data we collect

Day & Night Live Wallpaper has no sign-in and does not collect personal data on our servers. Here is what the app handles, and where:

Location
If you allow it, the app reads your device's location to work out sunrise, sunset and moon times on your device, and sends your coordinates to our weather provider to fetch the local forecast. The last known position is kept on your device only. We never receive or store it.
Photos you choose
Images you pick in Create are cropped and stored on your device. They are never uploaded.
Style preferences
Your scene, clock style, colors, fonts and other settings are stored locally on your device so your choices persist.
Purchase info
If you buy Day & Night Pro, Google Play handles the payment. We only receive confirmation that Pro is active, never your card details.
Ads (free version)
Google AdMob shows ads to free users, including optional rewarded ads you choose to watch. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, and personalize them where you allow it. Pro users see no ads.

Permissions we use

04

How we use your data

  • To run the app: drawing your scene for the current time and weather, and keeping your settings saved.
  • To unlock Day & Night Pro: checking your purchase with Google Play.
  • To show ads in the free version: through Google AdMob, as described below.
  • To respond to you: if you email us, we use your email and message only to reply.

We never use your photos or the location the app reads for weather for advertising, and we do not sell or share data with data brokers.

05

Third-party services

We rely on a small number of providers to run the app:

  • Open-Meteo: receives your coordinates to return the local weather forecast. No account or identifier is sent with them. See Open-Meteo's terms and privacy.
  • Google Play Billing: processes Day & Night Pro purchases. We never see your payment details.
  • Google AdMob: shows ads to free users and may collect the advertising ID and related data described above. See how Google uses data in ads.

Each provider has its own privacy practices, and we share only the minimum needed for the feature to work.

06

Storage & retention

Everything stays on your device. We don't keep copies on a server. If you clear the app's data or uninstall it, your photos, saved location, weather cache and settings are deleted with it.

07

Your choices & rights

  • You can use the whole app without creating an account, because there isn't one.
  • You can turn off location access at any time in your device's app permissions. The scene keeps running, but sun times and weather won't match where you are.
  • You can turn notifications off in the app's Settings or in your device's notification settings.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Settings → Google → Ads, which limits personalized ads across apps.
  • Upgrading to Day & Night Pro removes ads entirely.
  • You can delete all your data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.
  • If you've emailed us and want that conversation deleted, just ask.
08

Children's privacy

Day & Night Live Wallpaper is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we'll remove it promptly.
