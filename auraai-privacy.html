01

Overview

AuraAI, published by Softlight Studio Apps, is an AI assistant app: chat, translation, image creation, background removal, voice conversation, prayer times, Qibla direction, nearby mosques and routes.

The short version: there is no account. Your chats, photos and settings are stored on your phone. To answer you, the text you send (and a photo, if you attach one) passes through our server to an AI provider, which returns the answer. Our server does not keep your messages. We count how many free messages and pictures each install uses per day, using a random app ID, not your name or phone number. We don't sell your data.

02

Data the app handles

Messages & photos you send
When you chat, translate or ask about a photo, that text or photo and recent messages of the same chat are sent to our server, which forwards them to an AI provider and returns the answer. Our server doesn't store the content.
Chats on your phone
Your chats, chat titles and pictures are saved on your phone. Android may back up chats and settings to your own Google Drive (see Storage).
Random app ID & daily counts
The app creates a random ID on first launch. Our server stores this ID with the date and how many free messages and HD pictures were used that day, to apply the daily free limits.
Voice
Speech is turned into text by your phone's own speech service (for example Google). Aura's spoken answers may be created by a cloud voice service from the answer text; otherwise your phone's voice reads them.
Location
If you allow it, your location is used on your phone for prayer times and Qibla, and sent to map services to find nearby mosques, show maps and plan routes. It is not sent to our server.
Image prompts
Descriptions you give to Create image are sent to an image service (through our server, or directly from your phone to the free backup service) to make the picture.

Background removal runs entirely on your phone; the photo is not uploaded.

04

How we use data

  • To answer you: your message goes to an AI provider and the answer comes back to your phone.
  • To keep the app free and fair: daily counts per random app ID apply the free limits.
  • To keep the service working: our server logs which AI provider answered and how long it took (not what you wrote), to fix problems and pick the fastest provider.
  • To respond to you: if you email us or report an answer, we use your email and message only for that.

We do not use your chats for advertising, we do not sell data, and we do not share data with data brokers.

05

Third-party services

AuraAI uses free plans of these providers. Each gets only what its feature needs:

  • Supabase: hosts our server function and the daily counts.
  • AI providers (one answers each message, others are backups): Groq, Google (Gemini and Gemma), OpenRouter, Cloudflare Workers AI, Cerebras, GitHub Models and Mistral. Some free plans may use the content sent to them to improve their AI (for example Google's free Gemini API and Mistral's free plan), so please don't share private or sensitive details in chats.
  • Voice: Google (Gemini text-to-speech) or Groq may turn answer text into speech.
  • Pictures: Cloudflare Workers AI (FLUX.1 model) through our server; Pollinations.ai as a free backup, contacted directly from your phone (it sees your IP address and the description).
  • Maps & places: OpenStreetMap services (map tiles, Nominatim and Photon search, Overpass mosque search, OSRM routing) and CARTO map tiles receive the map area or coordinates needed.
  • Google ML Kit: background removal on your phone.
  • Google Drive (Android backup): if backup is on in your phone, Android stores the app's chats and settings in your own Google account.

Each provider has its own privacy policy. We send only the minimum needed for the feature to work.

06

Storage & retention

  • On your phone: chats are kept until you delete them, and are removed automatically after 90 days without use.
  • Our server: message content is not stored. Daily counts (random app ID, date, number) are deleted after 7 days. Service logs are kept for a short time by our hosting provider.
  • Backup: if Android backup is on, chats and settings (not pictures) are copied to your Google Drive and restored when you reinstall. You can also export chats to a file yourself in Settings.
07

Your choices & rights

  • You can use the whole app without an account, because there isn't one.
  • You can delete any chat, or all chats, in the app (Settings → Delete all chats).
  • You can export your chats to a file and import them on another phone (Settings → Chats).
  • You can turn microphone, location, camera and notification access off at any time in your phone's settings.
  • You can turn off Android backup in your phone's settings (Google → Backup).
  • You can delete everything on your phone by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.
  • You can ask us to delete the daily counts for your install, or any email conversation, by contacting us.
08

Children's privacy

AuraAI is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we'll remove it promptly.
