01

Overview

Depth Wallpaper, published by Softlight Studio, turns your photos into depth-aware live wallpapers with a customizable clock, date, quote, and widgets. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app.

The short version: there are no accounts, and your photos and edits never leave your device. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps.

02

Data we collect

Depth Wallpaper has no sign-in and does not collect personal data on our servers. Here is what the app handles, and where:

Photos you choose
Images you pick to build a wallpaper are processed entirely on your device (including the subject cutout). They are never uploaded.
Style preferences
Clock style, colors, fonts, saved presets, and your creations are stored locally on your device so your edits persist.
Approximate location
Only if you add the Weather widget. Your approximate location is sent to our weather provider to get current conditions. It is not stored by us.
Step count
Only if you add the Steps widget. Read from your phone's step sensor and kept on your device. It is never uploaded.
Purchase info
If you buy Premium, Google Play handles the payment. We only receive confirmation that Premium is unlocked, never your card details.
Wallpaper catalog
The app downloads wallpapers from our content server. These requests include standard technical data (like your IP address) that any web request carries, but no account or personal profile.

04

How we use your data

  • To run the app: rendering your wallpaper, keeping your edits saved, and showing widgets you add.
  • To unlock Premium: checking your purchase with Google Play.
  • To respond to you: if you email us through Contact Us, we use your email and message only to reply.

We do not use your photos, location, or activity data for advertising, and we do not sell or share data with data brokers.

05

Third-party services

We rely on a small number of providers to run the app:

  • Supabase: hosts the wallpaper catalog the app downloads. No personal data is sent to it.
  • Google Play Billing: processes the one-time Premium purchase. We never see your payment details.
  • Open-Meteo: receives your approximate location when the Weather widget is in use, to return current weather.
  • Google ML Kit: performs the subject cutout on your device. Your photos are not sent to Google.

Each provider has its own privacy practices, and we share only the minimum needed for the feature to work.

06

Storage & retention

Everything you create stays on your device. We don't keep copies on a server. If you clear the app's data or uninstall it, your locally stored wallpapers, presets, and settings are deleted with it.

07

Your choices & rights

  • You can use the whole app without creating an account, because there isn't one.
  • You can turn off location or physical activity access at any time in your device's app permissions. The Weather or Steps widget will simply stop updating.
  • You can delete all your data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.
  • If you've emailed us and want that conversation deleted, just ask.
08

Children's privacy

Depth Wallpaper is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we'll remove it promptly.

09

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the "Effective date" above and, for significant changes, note it in the app's release notes.
