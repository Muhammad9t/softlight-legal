01

Overview

Depth Clock Editor & Wallpaper, published by Softlight Studio Apps, turns your photos into depth-aware live wallpapers with a customizable clock, date, quote, and widgets. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app.

The short version: there are no accounts, and your photos and edits never leave your device. We don't sell your data. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID (see below); Depth Pro removes ads entirely.

02

Data we collect

Depth Clock Editor & Wallpaper has no sign-in and does not collect personal data on our servers. Here is what the app handles, and where:

Photos you choose
Images you pick to build a wallpaper are processed entirely on your device (including the subject cutout). They are never uploaded.
Style preferences
Clock style, colors, fonts, saved presets, and your creations are stored locally on your device so your edits persist.
Approximate location
Only if you add the Weather widget. Your approximate location is sent to our weather provider to get current conditions. It is not stored by us.
Step count
Only if you add the Steps widget. Read from your phone's step sensor and kept on your device. It is never uploaded.
Purchase info
If you buy Depth Pro (a subscription or the lifetime unlock), Google Play handles the payment. We only receive confirmation that Pro is active, never your card details.
Ads (free version)
Google AdMob shows ads to free users. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, and personalize them where you allow it. Pro users see no ads and no ad SDK requests are made for them.
Wallpaper catalog
The app downloads wallpapers from our content server. These requests include standard technical data (like your IP address) that any web request carries, but no account or personal profile.

04

How we use your data

  • To run the app: rendering your wallpaper, keeping your edits saved, and showing widgets you add.
  • To unlock Depth Pro: checking your purchase or subscription with Google Play.
  • To show ads in the free version: through Google AdMob, as described below.
  • To respond to you: if you email us through Contact Us, we use your email and message only to reply.

We never use your photos, your Weather-widget location, or your step data for advertising, and we do not sell or share data with data brokers.

05

Third-party services

We rely on a small number of providers to run the app:

  • Supabase: hosts the wallpaper catalog the app downloads. No personal data is sent to it.
  • Google Play Billing: processes Depth Pro subscriptions and the lifetime purchase. We never see your payment details.
  • Google AdMob: shows ads to free users and may collect the advertising ID and related data described above. See how Google uses data in ads.
  • Open-Meteo: receives your approximate location when the Weather widget is in use, to return current weather.
  • Google ML Kit: performs the subject cutout on your device. Your photos are not sent to Google.

Each provider has its own privacy practices, and we share only the minimum needed for the feature to work.

06

Storage & retention

Everything you create stays on your device. We don't keep copies on a server. If you clear the app's data or uninstall it, your locally stored wallpapers, presets, and settings are deleted with it.

07

Your choices & rights

  • You can use the whole app without creating an account, because there isn't one.
  • You can turn notifications off with the Notifications switch in Settings, or per type ("New wallpapers", "Wallpaper status") in your device's notification settings.
  • You can turn off location or physical activity access at any time in your device's app permissions. The Weather or Steps widget will simply stop updating.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change that choice any time in Settings → "Ad privacy choices".
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Settings → Google → Ads, which limits personalized ads across apps.
  • Upgrading to Depth Pro removes ads entirely.
  • You can delete all your data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.
  • If you've emailed us and want that conversation deleted, just ask.
08

Children's privacy

Depth Clock Editor & Wallpaper is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we'll remove it promptly.
