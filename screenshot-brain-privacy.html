AI Screenshot Brain — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026 · Softlight Studio Apps

1. Overview

AI Screenshot Brain is designed to work 100% offline on your device. The app does not declare Android's INTERNET permission, does not connect to any server, and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

2. Data Processed On-Device

All processing happens locally on your Android phone:

  • OCR, Image Labeling & QR/Barcode Scanning: Powered by on-device Google ML Kit models. Your screenshots never leave your phone.
  • Search Index, Notes, Folders & Expenses: Stored exclusively in a local SQLite database inside the app's private storage on your device.
  • Private Vault: Protected locally using Android's biometric/device-credential prompt (local_auth). Biometric data is handled solely by the Android OS and is never accessible to the app.

3. Permissions Used

  • Photos / Media (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Optional permission used only to scan your device's Screenshots folder so new screenshots are indexed automatically. If you decline, you can still use the Android Photo Picker or Share Sheet.
  • Biometrics (USE_BIOMETRIC): Used only when you lock or unlock your Private Vault.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used only for local reminders you explicitly set on a screenshot.

4. Third-Party Services & Analytics

AI Screenshot Brain contains no ads, no analytics SDKs, no crash-reporting uploads, and no cloud accounts.

5. Deleting Your Data

Because all indexed text, notes, folders, and expense records live only on your device, you can delete them anytime inside the app, clear app storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, email softlightstudioapps@gmail.com.
